Features

Everything Windy~Sync does

One small Rust app on both PCs that makes the new one feel like your old one.

DEVICE

Each PC draws itself

On the Device tab you name this PC and choose how it is drawn: a desktop monitor or a laptop, in graphite, black, silver or white. All drawings are original shapes, no company logos.

CONNECT

The PCs on your account

Find devices on this account lists every Windows PC registered in your bus repo. Pick one, choose to send or to receive, and the other PC shows a request its owner approves or declines.

PICK

A tree you tick

The sender scans what it has. The receiver sees it as a tree grouped by kind, with where each item lands; ticking a group ticks everything in it.

MOVE

Straight across, and it resumes

Only the picks travel, PC to PC over your network, as an authenticated XChaCha20-Poly1305 stream. A minute without a byte counts as a cut, and the receiver asks again for what has not arrived.

BRIEF

A brief your agent can act on

Each item lands where Windows keeps it on the new PC, and what was there is kept as .before-windysync. Then the receiver writes GOAL.md: what landed where, the problems, paths to rewrite and the winget, Scoop, npm, cargo and pipx lists to install from.
