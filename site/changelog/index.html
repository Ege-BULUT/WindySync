0.1.0

2026-11-27
  • WindySync starts from AnySync's engine and window with Windows on both ends: only Windows PCs are listed and accepted.
  • Its own window, shaped like a gust of wind, in an aqua palette with the WYSY logo.
  • Each PC gets an id, a name, a look (desktop or laptop, in four colours) and a key pair.
  • A private GitHub repo as the bus: devices, requests and transfers, with anything private sealed.
  • The item catalogue under Windows' own folders, and lists made with winget, Scoop, npm, cargo, pipx and the editors.
  • The encrypted PC to PC stream, resuming after a cut, and GOAL.md for your agent.
  • CI tests and builds on Windows; tagging a version releases windysync-windows-x64.exe.
